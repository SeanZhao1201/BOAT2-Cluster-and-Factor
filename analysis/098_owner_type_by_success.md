**Chart type:** Sunburst (inner ring: Public/Private, outer ring: Success level)

## Cross-Tabulation Summary

### Cluster 1 (n=3, all Private)
| Success Level | Private |
|---------------|---------|
| Very Successful | 3 (100%) |

All 3 private-sector projects report "very successful" outcomes.

### Cluster 2 (n=24, Public 54.2% / Private 45.8%)
| Success Level | Public (n=13) | Private (n=11) |
|---------------|--------------|----------------|
| Very Successful | 8 (61.5%) | 7 (63.6%) |
| More Successful | 2 (15.4%) | 3 (27.3%) |
| Moderately Successful | 1 (7.7%) | 0 (0%) |
| More Unsuccessful | 1 (7.7%) | 0 (0%) |
| Very Unsuccessful | 1 (7.7%) | 1 (9.1%) |

Both public and private sectors report high success rates (~77-91% successful). This cluster's collaborative, participatory decision-making style correlates with positive outcomes regardless of ownership.

### Cluster 3 (n=53, Public 75.5% / Private 24.5%)
| Success Level | Public (n=40) | Private (n=13) |
|---------------|--------------|----------------|
| Very Successful | 14 (35.0%) | 6 (46.2%) |
| More Successful | 9 (22.5%) | 3 (23.1%) |
| Moderately Successful | 13 (32.5%) | 2 (15.4%) |
| More Unsuccessful | 1 (2.5%) | 0 (0%) |
| Very Unsuccessful | 3 (7.5%) | 2 (15.4%) |

Public sector shows a wider spread of outcomes, with 32.5% only "moderately successful." Private sector is slightly more polarized - higher "very successful" rate (46.2%) but also higher "very unsuccessful" rate (15.4%).

### Cluster 4 (n=28, Public 50% / Private 50%)
| Success Level | Public (n=14) | Private (n=14) |
|---------------|--------------|----------------|
| Very Successful | 1 (7.1%) | 1 (7.1%) |
| More Successful | 6 (42.9%) | 2 (14.3%) |
| Moderately Successful | 5 (35.7%) | 6 (42.9%) |
| More Unsuccessful | 1 (7.1%) | 4 (28.6%) |
| Very Unsuccessful | 1 (7.1%) | 1 (7.1%) |

The lowest-performing cluster overall. **Private sector fares worse** - 28.6% report "more unsuccessful" vs only 7.1% for public. Public sector concentrates on "more successful" (42.9%), while private concentrates on "moderately successful" (42.9%). This cluster's hierarchical, low-flexibility style appears to hurt private sector projects more severely.

## Key Findings

1. **Cluster 2 achieves the best outcomes for both sectors** - Its collaborative, participatory decision-making correlates with >60% "very successful" for both public and private.
2. **Cluster 4's hierarchical style hurts private sector more** - Private projects in this cluster have 35.7% unsuccessful outcomes vs 14.2% for public, suggesting private organizations suffer more from rigid, command-oriented decision-making.
3. **Public sector in Cluster 3 has moderate but stable outcomes** - The "moderate mainstream" approach produces consistently middling results (32.5% moderately successful), neither excellent nor poor.
4. **Ownership type interacts with decision-making style to influence success** - The same cluster can produce very different outcomes for public vs private owners (especially Cluster 4), indicating that the relationship between organizational decision-making and project success is moderated by ownership type.
