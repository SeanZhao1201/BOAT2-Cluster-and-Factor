## Key Findings

1. **Public sector gravitates toward traditional PDMs** - DBB and DB dominate public projects, especially in Cluster 3.
2. **Private sector shows more IPD adoption** - IPD appears almost exclusively in private sector projects (Clusters 1 and 2).
3. **Cluster 3's public dominance coincides with traditional delivery** - The largest cluster combines public ownership with conventional delivery methods and moderate decision-making.
4. **Cluster 2's private sector is most innovative** - Private projects in Cluster 2 show the highest IPD concentration (36.4% of private projects in that cluster).
