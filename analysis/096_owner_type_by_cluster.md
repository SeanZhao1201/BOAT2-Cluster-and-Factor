## Key Findings

1. **Cluster 1** is entirely Private sector (100%), though sample size is very small (n=3).
2. **Cluster 3** is dominated by Public/Government projects (75.5%), making it the most distinctly public-sector cluster. This aligns with its "moderate mainstream" decision-making profile - public organizations tend toward more standardized, moderate approaches.
3. **Clusters 2 and 4** show near-even splits (~50/50), suggesting that the decision-making characteristics defining these clusters cut across the public/private boundary.
4. The public sector is overrepresented in the overall sample (62%) relative to private (38%), consistent with construction industry survey demographics where public owners are often more accessible for academic research.
